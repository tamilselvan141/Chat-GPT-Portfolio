User flow

How the product works

Core journey

  1. 1A meaningful moment is created or occurs.
  2. 2An employee discovers it in a relevant context.
  3. 3They join a short, clearly bounded interaction.
  4. 4Recognition or support follows.
  5. 5Leaving is as easy as joining.

What was simplified

  • Moment-based context
  • Visible availability
  • Short interactions
  • Easy join, skip and exit actions
  • Reporting and safety controls

Every interaction is designed to be short and optional, so participating never feels like a commitment.

User experience & trust

How the experience should feel

Before

  • Pressure to participate in organised activities
  • Social hesitation with unfamiliar colleagues
  • Worry about interrupting someone mid-work

Experience goals

WarmWelcomingSafeLow-pressureEffortless

Availability is explicit, interactions are visibly short, and skip and exit are given the same prominence as join so there is no social cost to declining.

User behaviour

How the design supports user behaviour

  1. 1A meaningful moment occurs
  2. 2An employee discovers the moment
  3. 3The employee participates
  4. 4Recognition or support occurs

The product aims to replace obligation-driven participation with a voluntary loop that gets easier each time it is repeated.

Key decisions

The choices that shaped the product

01

Moments instead of events

Engagement is anchored to something happening right now, which gives the interaction a reason to exist and a natural end point.

02

Making availability easy to see

Interviews surfaced fear of interrupting as a recurring blocker, so availability is shown before any interaction is initiated.

03

Exit is as easy as entry

Join, skip and exit share equal visual weight, which lowers the perceived risk of taking part.

04

Safety built into the MVP

Reporting and controls were scoped into the first version rather than deferred, because psychological safety is a precondition for participation.

STAR summary

The project in four parts

situation

Employee engagement largely depended on scheduled activities and formal initiatives that sometimes felt forced and provided limited opportunities for natural everyday connection.

task

Explore how workplace engagement could become more accessible, meaningful and low-effort without interrupting employees' normal work.

action

Conducted qualitative employee interviews, synthesized behavioural insights, created HMW statements, prioritized an MVP, and designed employee and admin experiences from concept through high-fidelity prototype.

result

Created a 0→1 employee engagement concept centered on low-effort interactions and meaningful workplace moments. The concept was presented at a Singapore business expo and generated prospective interest and feedback.

Reflection

What I learned

  • Interviews consistently pointed at social risk rather than lack of time, which changed the product from a scheduling problem into a safety and signalling problem.
  • Designing the exit path carefully did more for participation than any incentive mechanic would have.

Future scope

What I would validate next

  • Usability testing with employees in a live workplace.
  • Validate whether repeated participation actually increases over time.
  • Admin insights that do not compromise employee privacy.
