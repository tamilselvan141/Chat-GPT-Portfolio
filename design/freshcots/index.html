Key decisions

The choices that shaped the product

01

Capability before career history

The profile leads with what a candidate can do, so people with gaps or non-linear paths are not filtered out before they are seen.

02

Video as structured evidence

Video screening is presented as a consistent, comparable step rather than free-form media, so it helps recruiters instead of adding noise.

03

Two devices, two layouts

The candidate experience is designed mobile-first while the recruiter workspace is a dense desktop layout — the same data recomposed for very different tasks.

04

Progress visible to both sides

Application status is shown to candidates to reduce the uncertainty that drives mass applying.

STAR summary

The project in four parts

situation

Traditional job portals often favor conventional career paths and resume-based evaluation, which can disadvantage candidates with limited experience, career transitions or gaps.

task

Design a 0→1 hiring platform that allowed candidates to demonstrate more than a resume while helping recruiters discover and evaluate relevant talent efficiently.

action

Designed the experience across candidate, expert and employer modules, including research, flows, wireframes, interface design, prototyping, candidate discovery, screening and video-based evaluation.

result

Delivered a scalable, development-ready platform that reached beta / live implementation and gave candidates additional ways to demonstrate potential beyond traditional resume information.

Reflection

What I learned

  • Designing for two opposed incentives — candidates wanting visibility and recruiters wanting fewer profiles — meant most decisions were trade-offs, not improvements for one side.
  • Video only works as evidence when it is structured; unstructured media would have made screening slower, not faster.

Future scope

What I would validate next

  • Usability testing with candidates and recruiters in beta.
  • Measure screening time and shortlist quality once live data exists.
  • Matching that stays explainable to both sides.
